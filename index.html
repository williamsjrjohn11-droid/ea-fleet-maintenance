Log Fuel

Tap the photo box to snap the receipt (photo just attaches to the entry for now — you still type in the numbers below it). Pick the truck, enter gallons and price per gallon and the total fills in on its own — you can overwrite it if the pump's total doesn't match. Odometer and location help the Dashboard track mileage-based maintenance, so fill those in when you can. Tap Save Fuel Entry when done.

Log Maintenance

Same pattern as fuel — pick the truck, pick a Service Type from the dropdown (Oil Change, Tire Rotation, DOT Inspection, Brake Inspection, Chassis Lube, or Other). Picking one of the first five matches it against that truck's maintenance schedule, so the Dashboard's due-countdown resets automatically. "Other" logs fine but won't get a due-reminder.

Dashboard

One card per truck, showing current odometer, last fuel-up, and every tracked service with a colored dot: green = not due soon, amber = due within 14 days or the last 10% of the mileage interval, red = overdue. These are computed automatically from your Log Maintenance entries — nothing to update by hand.

Reports

Fuel + maintenance cost totals per truck, added up from every entry you've logged. Excel export is planned but not built yet.

Adjusting maintenance intervals

The 5 default intervals (Oil Change, Tire Rotation, DOT Inspection, Brake Inspection, Chassis Lube) live in the MaintenanceRules tab of the shared Google Sheet. To change one truck's interval without affecting the rest of the fleet, add a new row with that truck's number filled in — it overrides the blank/default row for that truck only.

Text Alerts

When anything's due soon or overdue, the banner at the top gets a Text Alert button. Tapping it opens your phone's own Messages app with the due list already filled in — you review it and hit send, same as the "Text driver" button in Dispatch Bridge. Nothing is sent automatically. Add the numbers it should be able to text below (usually management, who can then relay it to drivers).
